# TECH 112 — AI in Industry (Applied AI & Workflow Automation)

### Course & Lab Overview
During **TECH 112 (AI in Industry)** at **DeVry University**, I completed guided hands-on labs exploring how generative AI, structured prompt engineering, and workflow automation support everyday business and IT tasks—while practicing privacy safeguards and accuracy verification.

---

### Tools & Concepts Practiced
- **AI & Automation Tools:** ChatGPT (OpenAI LLM), DALL·E (text-to-image prompting), and **n8n** (simulated workflow automation)
- **Prompt Engineering Styles:** Interrogative, imperative, declarative, and conversational prompting
- **Prompt Constraints & Refinement:** Controlling output length, formatting, domain boundaries, and privacy rules through iterative revisions
- **Responsible AI & Governance:** Evaluating bias, hallucinations, data privacy, transparency, and human oversight

---

### Completed Coursework Activities
1. **Prompt Engineering & Iterative Refinement:** Practiced adding structured constraints and follow-up revisions to fix vague, incomplete, or inconsistent AI outputs.
2. **Generative AI Workplace & Media Analysis:** Tested multi-part prompts evaluating both the productivity benefits and the risks of over-relying on AI-generated content across writing, design, and media workflows.
3. **Text-to-Image Prompting (DALL·E):** Built detailed visual descriptions to control image composition, style, and accuracy through text instructions.
4. **Simulated Calendar Workflow Automation (`n8n`):** Completed a simulated workflow automation lab routing calendar-related tasks and identified an ambiguous email destination (`youremail@gmail.com`), flagging the need to verify data routing before connecting live accounts.
5. **Guided AI Agent Lab:** Completed an introductory AI agent building exercise exploring how automated tools execute multi-step tasks.

---

### Key Takeaways & Responsible AI Practices
- **Verify Before Trusting:** Clear constraints reduce ambiguity, but AI outputs must still be fact-checked against reliable sources before being used in business or IT operations.
- **Data Privacy First:** Always verify where email addresses, credentials, or internal records are routed before connecting live accounts to an automation workflow.
- **Honest Technical Reporting:** Clearly distinguish between simulated lab environments and live production deployments when documenting technical work.
- **Human Oversight:** AI speeds up drafting and routine workflows, but human judgment remains essential for security, ethics, and final decisions.
